VIII · Questions

Straight answers.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No one honestly can: Google decides rankings. Every site we build has the search basics done properly. Ongoing SEO is something we will offer only once we can do it well.

Do you do SEO, social media or ads?

Not yet. Today we design, build and improve websites. Search and AI visibility and monthly growth plans come later, once our websites are proven.

Who owns the website?

Your domain is registered in your name and your content is yours. We agree hosting and handover in writing before we start.

Do you build in Arabic?

Yes. Every site we build works in Arabic and English, with Arabic reading right to left, and a native speaker reads the Arabic before launch.

Where are you based?

In Amman, Jordan. For now we work with businesses in Amman. We will take clients from further away once our work here is proven.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what your business needs, so we do not publish prices. After the first look, you get a written scope and a clear price before anything starts.

Are you a new company?

Yes. Our first sites are shown on this page, labelled honestly as live trials and previews. If you work with us now, you will be one of our first clients, and we will treat you that way.
